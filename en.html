Full-Stack Analytics Repository & Centralized Reporting Platform

flag The Challenge

Marketing impact measurement and performance reporting operated across fragmented, siloed channels (Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn, GA4, Google Search Console, Mailchimp, and Livestorm). Managing multiple accounts required disconnected manual spreadsheets with disparate visual styles across different channels and team members, projecting a fragmented brand presence, slowing delivery, hindering funnel analysis, and lacking a unified repository and consistent corporate design standard.

lightbulb The Developed Solution

Leveraging advanced Prompt Engineering and Claude-assisted full-stack development, a comprehensive production-grade web platform was designed and built from scratch, powered by a modern stack: React 18 + Vite, Tailwind CSS, Recharts, a Supabase backend database, and automated CI/CD deployment via GitHub and Vercel:

  • check_circle Unified Central Repository: An all-in-one centralized intelligence hub allowing team members to look up performance metrics and reports across all clients and channels in seconds.
  • check_circle Visual Unification & Single Business Identity: Eliminates aesthetic disparities across department deliverables by standardizing charts, typography, and reporting layouts, projecting a cohesive, professional brand identity as a single integrated business unit.
  • check_circle Comprehensive 5-Pillar Architecture: Unifies multi-channel tracking across Social Media, Paid Media, Email Marketing, Webinars, and Website. In turn, each pillar breaks down into dedicated sub-pillars depending on the channel (for example, within Website, traffic analytics via Google Analytics 4 is segmented from organic SEO performance via Google Search Console; in Paid Media, Google Ads vs. Meta Ads).
  • check_circle Dual Export Engine (Internal vs. External): Tailored reporting outputs based on audience: external downloads provide clean, executive summaries directly for clients, while internal downloads supply comprehensive operational variables and granular diagnostic data for the marketing team.
  • check_circle Dynamic Client & Date Filtering: Frictionless exploration with account-level views and a cross-channel report combiner for executive audits.
  • check_circle Interactive Recharts Visualizations: Real-time trend graphs, detailed KPI breakdowns, and standardized technical glossaries ensuring institutional metric integrity.
insights

trending_up The Impact

Consolidated an authoritative operational, intelligence, and unified reporting backbone. Eliminated data dispersion, aesthetic discrepancies across deliverables, and manual spreadsheet assembly, accelerating client reporting cycles and providing leadership with a clear, comparable, and visually cohesive overview of marketing performance.

Interactive Template for Professional Lead Database Delivery

flag The Challenge

Delivering B2B prospect lists to sales teams typically involves handing over dense, static spreadsheets (Excel/CSV). This creates significant operational friction: SDRs waste valuable time manually filtering records, searching for individual LinkedIn profiles, and struggling to quickly identify high-priority decision-makers. Furthermore, sales reps are forced to improvise cold outreach copy from scratch, leading to fragmented brand messaging and diminished response rates.

lightbulb The Solution Developed with Gemini

Using an advanced Prompt Engineering framework with Google Gemini, an adaptable and reusable single-file dashboard template was developed. The workflow is streamlined: users simply paste raw extracted lead data into Gemini, define their campaign outreach goals, and specify the company they are representing. Gemini immediately generates a fully interactive, production-ready sales cockpit featuring:

  • check_circle Multi-Column Dynamic Filtering: Instant client-side filtering by Job Title/Role (e.g., Sustainability, Operations, Procurement), Company, and Country, paired with real-time KPI cards (Total Contacts, LinkedIn Profiles, Companies, Countries).
  • check_circle Direct LinkedIn & CRM Routing: Each contact row includes a 1-click LinkedIn profile launcher for frictionless research, plus direct quick-links to the corporate CRM.
  • check_circle Dynamic Filtered Excel Export: Allows immediate download of the refined dataset reflecting all currently active search and column filters.
  • check_circle AI Prospecting Templates: Copy-ready, channel-optimized outreach scripts generated for Cold Email (catchy subject lines & pain-point value propositions), LinkedIn connection notes, and Phone / WhatsApp pitches.
  • check_circle Dark / Light Mode Toggle: User-friendly interface with instant theme switching to support extended outbound prospecting sessions.
groups

trending_up The Impact

Accelerates SDR outbound velocity by turning flat contact databases into living sales enablement tools in minutes. Eliminates cold-email writer's block, standardizes outbound quality across the commercial pipeline, and drives higher initial contact response rates.

Marketing & Communications Services Dynamic Pricing Estimator

* All pricing figures, rates, and estimates displayed on screen and in the video are fictitious, crafted exclusively for demonstration purposes to showcase the tool's capabilities.

flag The Challenge

Scoping and budgeting marketing services across regional business units and internal or external stakeholders typically involved lengthy email back-and-forths. Each request required fragmented manual calculations, caused approval bottlenecks, and led to friction due to a lack of transparency regarding the operational criteria that shift deliverable costs (rush turnarounds, graphic complexity, or multilingual production).

lightbulb The Solution Developed with Gemini

Leveraging Google Gemini Prompt Engineering, a responsive single-page pricing calculator was engineered, empowering stakeholders and clients to self-estimate project investments in real time:

  • check_circle 8 Centralized Operational Pillars: Modular scoping covering Graphic Design, Social Media, In-Person Events, Virtual Events (Webinars), Campaigns & Paid Media, CRM, Website & SEO, Benchmarking, and Reporting.
  • check_circle Live Dynamic Pricing Engine: Quantity-based inputs with instant recalculation of min/max cost range boundaries and overall estimated average cost on a sticky summary bar.
  • check_circle Built-In Commercial Logic & Volume Discounts: Automated scaling rules, such as automatic price drops for email sequences of 3+ emails and tiered pricing for Paid Media by platform scope (Search, PMax, or 360°).
  • check_circle Terms & Pricing Variables Sub-View: Interactive sub-landing detailing the exact operational criteria that guide project fees (under-48h rush fees, custom graphic complexity, voiceover, multilingual requirements, or third-party vendors).
payments

trending_up The Impact

Eliminated commercial scoping bottlenecks. Stakeholders and clients can forecast and plan budgets autonomously within seconds, establishing full operational transparency and drastically expediting project approvals and campaign kickoff cycles.
